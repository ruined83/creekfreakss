# Creek Freaks - Site Audit & Ideas

## 1. "Living" Social Feed 📸
**Problem:** The site feels a bit static. "Nature" is visual, but the content is text-heavy.
**Idea:** Add a **Dynamic Instagram/Photo Grid** to the Home or Community page.
- **Why:** Shows real people fishing/hiking.
- **How:** A scrolling masonry grid of "Recent Catches" or "Peaceful Moments" tagged with `#CreekFreaks`.

## 2. Interactive Memorial Submission 🕯️
**Problem:** The "Memorial Wall" is great, but how do people add to it?
**Idea:** Add a **"Submit a Tribute"** form directly on the Memorial page.
- **Why:** Increases engagement. People want to share *their* stories.
- **How:** A simple form (Name, Photo, Short Story) that goes to "Pending" state for you to approve in Admin.

## 3. "The Tackle Box" (Newsletter) ✉️
**Problem:** "Subscribe" is generic.
**Idea:** Rebrand the newsletter as **"The Tackle Box"** or **"Weekly Cast"**.
- **Offer:** "Get 5 tips for finding peace on the water" or "Weekly mental health check-ins."
- **Why:** Gives a *reason* to sign up beyond just "updates."

## 4. Audio "Soundscapes" 🎧
**Problem:** You have a podcast, but the site is silent.
**Idea:** Add an optional **Background Ambience** toggle (birds, flowing water).
- **Why:** Immediate immersion. Fits the "Mental Health/Peace" theme perfectly.
